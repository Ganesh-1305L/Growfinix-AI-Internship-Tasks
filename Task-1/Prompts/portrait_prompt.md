# Prompt 1: Ultra-Realistic DSLR Portrait

## Objective
To generate an ultra-realistic professional DSLR portrait using advanced prompt engineering techniques.

## AI Model
**Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large (8B)**

Model: https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-3.5-large

## Positive Prompt
Generate an ultra-realistic professional DSLR portrait of a fictional 24-year-old woman with natural facial features, realistic skin pores, detailed eyes, and dark hair. She is wearing an elegant cream-colored outfit. Head-and-shoulders composition, eye-level camera angle, 85mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, shallow depth of field, soft diffused window lighting, natural shadows, smooth background bokeh, accurate skin tones, realistic fabric texture, sharp focus on the eyes, professional editorial photography, ultra-realistic details, high resolution, natural color grading.


## Prompt Engineering Techniques
- **Subject description:** Specified the subject's appearance, age, hair, and clothing.
- **Camera terminology:** Used 85mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, and shallow depth of field to guide the photographic style.
- **Lighting:** Described soft window lighting and natural shadows.
- **Texture and detail:** Emphasized realistic skin pores, eyes, and fabric.


## Expected Result
A photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait with natural skin texture, sharp eyes, soft background blur, and professional lighting.
