# Prompt 2: Cinematic Promotional Poster

## Objective
To create a cinematic promotional poster for a fictional science-fiction thriller using advanced prompt engineering techniques.

## AI Model
**Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large (8B)**

Model: https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-3.5-large

## Positive Prompt
Create a photorealistic cinematic promotional poster for a fictional science-fiction thriller titled "THE LAST HORIZON". A lone astronaut stands on the edge of a vast alien desert, facing a gigantic futuristic city beneath a mysterious eclipsed planet. Wide-angle low-angle camera shot, strong foreground silhouette, immense scale, layered atmospheric fog, floating dust particles, realistic weathered spacesuit fabric, scratched metal surfaces, intricate architectural details, dramatic orange sunset light from the horizon, contrasting deep teal shadows, volumetric light rays, subtle lens flare, cinematic color grading, rich contrast, deep blacks, controlled highlights, atmospheric depth, professional blockbuster poster composition, portrait orientation, highly detailed, photorealistic. Leave clear space at the top for a movie title and at the bottom for credits.


## Prompt Engineering Techniques
- **Subject and setting:** Described the astronaut, alien desert, futuristic city, and eclipsed planet.
- **Composition:** Specified a low-angle shot, foreground silhouette, and portrait orientation.
- **Lighting:** Used dramatic orange sunset light and contrasting teal shadows.
- **Atmosphere:** Included fog, floating dust, volumetric light rays, and atmospheric depth.
- **Color grading:** Specified cinematic colors, deep blacks, and rich contrast.


## Expected Result
A detailed, photorealistic science-fiction poster with dramatic lighting, atmospheric depth, and professional cinematic composition.

`images/cinematic_poster.webp`
